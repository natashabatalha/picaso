About PICASO

What the underlying code can do. Some of it is not in this app yet; the documentation covers all of it.

  • Transmission spectroscopy of planets
  • Thermal emission spectroscopy of planets and brown dwarfs
  • Reflected light spectroscopy of planets
  • Phase curves of planets
  • 1D climate modeling of planets and brown dwarfs
  • Fitting models to data with retrievals
